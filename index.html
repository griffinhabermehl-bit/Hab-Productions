03 / Behind the Camera

The Person Behind Hab.

I'm Griffin Habermehl, the filmmaker and photographer behind Hab Productions, based in San Luis Obispo, California. I'm also a current student at Cal Poly San Luis Obispo.

I got my start in FPV drone racing and engineering in middle school, shaping the way I capture movement and perspective. From fast-paced action sports to commercial projects and photography, I love bringing creative ideas to life. When I'm not behind the camera, you'll find me surfing or rock climbing.
